
## 5. Welcome Splash Screen

A beautiful, respectful splash screen has been added for the **7 Jyotirlinga & 1 Dham Yatra**:
- **Branding**: Uses the provided Jaijaipur Sewa Samiti logo, sized perfectly without stretching.
- **Animations**: The logo elegantly scales into view, followed by a staggered fade-up of the welcome text.
- **Flow**: The splash screen covers the entire window and automatically fades out after 3 seconds, revealing the existing website underneath.
- **Behavior**: It is designed to appear when the user first opens or reloads the website, but will *not* annoyingly replay while they are clicking around between passenger pages and admin dashboards.
